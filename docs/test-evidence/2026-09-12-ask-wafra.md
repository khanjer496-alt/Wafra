# Ask Wafra: English-first improvements, 12 September 2026

Scope: the current working-tree assistant, its evidence view, and contextual
entrypoints. Additional localization is deferred. All test ledgers are synthetic.
These changes are excluded from the separate frozen TestFlight beta candidate.

## Result

- Fixed salary/business income and historical merchant-payment interpretation.
- Unknown, ambiguous, or unsupported conditions clarify without broadening totals.
- Preserved merchant/category/account/date filters, custom reporting years, and
  context from Home, Spending, and merchant screens.
- Exact transaction proof includes split contributions, effective cash funding
  dates/accounts, separate comparison groups, and ordered top-N purchase subsets.
- Immutable ledger changes and day changes invalidate old answers; refresh uses
  current records and clock. Erase/restore clears the session and pending input.
- Fixed composer placement, delayed scrolling until layout settles, and wrapping
  of long merchant choices on 320-point screens.
- Default Home order prioritizes due payments without changing saved preferences.

## Limits

Native keyboard behavior was not validated. The machine had no compatible
simulator app or Pods/workspace, a broken idb interpreter, and insufficient free
disk during native preparation. No physical-device or store-build claim follows
from web/hook tests. No paid build, model integration, or publication was
performed by this task.

Ask remains a bounded local query feature. Unsupported conditions are explicit;
it does not claim complete bank capture, universal bank parsing, or financial
advice. Currency tests include USD, JPY (zero decimals), and KWD (three decimals).
