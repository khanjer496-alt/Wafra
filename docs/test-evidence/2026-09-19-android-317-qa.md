## Performance interpretation

Build 314 reproduced substantial navigation delay during the real inbox scan. Eight screenshot-sampled tab-highlight upper bounds ranged from 0.93 to 9.16 seconds. One persistent defect also showed Spending content while Home was selected, with both scenes exposed; it persisted for 83.9 seconds without input. A controlled Spending-then-Home sequence recovered it without restarting.

The history scan completed naturally after checking 29,991 messages. It was not reset for benchmarking. Later builds were therefore measured with completed history. Build 317's normal tab-highlight upper bounds were approximately 0.52–1.36 seconds, including ADB and foreground-check overhead, followed by separate scene verification. These measurements do not establish a percentage improvement under the earlier active-import workload.

The checkpoint optimization is supported by independent host tests on a large ledger: repeated unchanged history pages retain collection identity and avoid repeated whole-ledger normalization, while the final page still performs canonical reconciliation. The navigation backport additionally has actual-source and real-React regression coverage; see [the DelayedFreeze evidence](2026-09-19-delayed-freeze-backport.md).

## Data retention and parser checks

A private snapshot comparison across the build-314 to build-315 update retained every transaction identifier, amount and direction in the more-than-14,000-row ledger. One account merge and four account-reference remaps matched an independently replayed cleanup. Installation identity was subsequently preserved through build 317.

An earlier cold launch changed displayed counts before a matching pre-launch snapshot existed. Existing normalization can explain that pattern, but the precise historical removal was not retrospectively certified. Older export comparisons are not substitutes for that missing snapshot. They separately identified four older security-challenge sources that parser 49 rejects; their removal cannot be attributed to that particular restart.

The older export comparison also found 147 changed local FX equivalents. Original foreign amounts and currencies were unchanged, and both snapshots' local equivalents matched their recorded rates arithmetically. Historical provider-rate provenance was not verified offline. Four foreign refund parses omitted FX metadata; their saved records already retained it. Parser 50 corrects future parsing while backfill version 49 remains unchanged, so this correction does not request a new history reread.

Thirteen synthetic preview cases were exercised on build 314, including English and Arabic, Arabic-Indic digits, thousands and sub-unit amounts, salary, statements, and four non-posting cases. Four targeted previews were then checked on build 317. Host verification for the final parser change passed 1,228 parser, 1,338 corpus, 34 invariant, 804 unit and 356 worker checks. The broader normal-path gate passed 76 app suites, three server suites, 1,143 interaction/workflow tests and 27 onboarding checks. Type checking and lint passed. The complete local macOS harness still stops at the absent iOS workspace after its native-history checks; it is not reported as a successful local iOS build.
