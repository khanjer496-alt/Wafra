## Safe area

Apple crops the header to narrower aspect ratios on smaller devices and
overlays the app icon, name and Get button along the bottom. The headline and
app name stay inside the centre 60% of the width and the top 72% of the
height. Phones may bleed off the bottom edge because that area is overlay.

## Content rules

- No price, trial or subscription terms in the art.
- No "AI" wording.
- Amounts are AED because the demo ledger is AED; they are sample data, not a
  coverage claim.
- The app has no photos of people, so the art uses no people imagery. Adding
  stock photography would show something the app does not.
- Check Apple's current header and search-visual specifications at upload
  time. Apple revises them when the hardware line changes.
