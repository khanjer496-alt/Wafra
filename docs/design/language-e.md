# Design language E — the foundation

Owner-approved on 2026-09-25. This file is the contract every screen workstream
builds on: the rules, the band each screen wears, the dark-mode mapping, and
every foundation API with a short example. Screens **use** these pieces; if one
is missing, add it in your own files and say so — do not fork the foundation.

Reference boards (design data, not code): `App-*.dc.html` and `App-*-Dark.dc.html`,
generated by `boards_app0.py`, `boards_app1.py`, `boards_app2.py`, `boards_e.py`
and `dark.py`. Home (`src/screens/journal-home-screen.tsx`) is the worked
example of everything below.

## The rules

- **Every tab owns a colour.** Home ink, Spending near-black (its category
  coins carry the colour), Bills ochre, Accounts slate. Flows (capture, review,
  add) are indigo; settings and data are sand.
  Detail screens take their parent's colour.
- **A screen is a band and a sheet.** The band holds the one figure that
  matters; the cream sheet (28pt top radius, overlapping the band by 28pt)
  holds the detail. Sheets and dialogs are the same surface, lifted.
- **Geist only.** No serif, no italics. Band figures are Geist SemiBold with
  tabular digits (`BandFigure`) — never Geist Mono, whose comma spaces out
  "5 , 480". Row amounts keep Geist Mono (`Money`, `TransactionRow`).
  Headlines say what the screen is or shows; no slogans.
- **One accent per screen** (its tab colour) plus the Wafra logo green
  `#1F6B52` for the one figure that is good news, with cream on it. As text on
  a dark band it lifts to `#6BC79E` (`accentOnBand`). Stat tiles use the band's
  own tone.
- **Colour means status, or a category on Spending.** Limits: blue within
  (`limitOk`), amber from 85%, red over (`StatusBar`, `limitStatus`). Success
  (done, paid, confirmed) is the logo green (`statusOk`). Spending gives each
  category its own colour (`categoryColor`, `src/lib/category-colors.ts`): its
  coin on the band and its glyph tile on the sheet match. Glyph tiles
  elsewhere stay one tone (`GlyphTile`).
- **Everything drawn is a tile from real figures**: merchant logo tiles (kept
  on every merchant row; the category glyph is only the fallback), glyph
  tiles, week bars, calendar cells, the share bar, timeline pins. No
  decorative shapes, no invented data.
- **The personal pattern** (`PatternMosaic`) appears only on onboarding,
  Home's header, the lock screen and the recap cover. It never carries money.
- **Tab bar.** Android and web: a floating ink pill; the selected tab shows its
  colour and name, the others are spoken icons. iOS: the system tab bar
  (NativeTabs), each item tinted with its tab colour.
- **Buttons** are plain: 56pt, 16pt radius, centred label, no arrow in a
  circle (`EButton`). Chips are 38pt pills (`BandChip`).
- **Motion.** Sheets rise 40pt on the 260/24 spring; bands never move; bars
  and pins grow from the baseline 50ms apart; money never spins. Reduce
  Motion (or a running screen reader) shows everything in place. Android keeps
  its measured motion bypasses. A group of coins that moves one after another
  (a stack, the day columns, an arriving row and its coin) runs on one
  native-driven clock (`useCoinClock`/`coinTrack` in `coin-motion.ts`): each
  coin reads its own window of it, shaped like the spring it replaces, never
  `Animated.delay`/`sequence` (JS timers). Home's coins hold their first reveal
  until Home can be seen (`useHomeRevealReady`: splash gone, App Lock cleared,
  app in the foreground); a change made while the app is in the background
  lands at once and never replays (`docs/performance/launch-resume-2026-10-05.md`).
- **Large text.** Every band and sheet layout stacks or wraps under
  `useLargeTextLayout()` (see `2026-09-25-large-text-audit.md`).
- **RTL.** Bands and sheets mirror (logical `start`/`end` everywhere; the back
  chevron flips in `Icon`). Figures stay LTR-isolated as today.
- **Honesty.** BRIEF.md's rules still apply in full: never invent data, never
  merge incomplete balances, never claim coverage the app does not have. No
  English sentence in `src/app` or `src/components`: copy lives in `src/lib/*-copy.ts`.

## Dark mode mapping (dark.py)

- Bands deepen so they never glare: ink `#0B0A08`, Spending `#0C0C0C`, ochre
  `#5E4719` (light text on it), slate `#1B3F4A`, indigo `#1D2148`, sand `#26221C`.
  Text on every dark band is `#F2EFE8`.
- Sheets `#1C1A16`, cards `#24211C`, rules `#3B362E`, text `#F2EFE8`,
  secondary `#A9A29A`, glyph ground `#2E2A23`.
- Glyphs, links and status move to lighter tints on dark surfaces: cream
  `#F2EFE8` (Spending, as Home), slate `#7FB4C4`, indigo `#A8B1F0`, green `#6BC79E`
  (success), blue `#8AB2EE` (within a limit), amber `#D9AE62`, red `#E08A70`
  (`tint`, `statusOk/Near/Over`, `limitOk`).
- Controls keep their accent fill; text set on an accent keeps its colour.
- The pattern keeps its fills on the ink band; sand grounds become `#2E2A23`
  and glyph strokes take the lighter tints (`PatternPalette.dark`).
- The segmented control's selected pill becomes the dark sheet with the band
  tint as its text. Spending's pill is cream with logo-green text in both
  schemes, as its band is near-black. Its sheet tint is ink (cream in dark),
  as Home's: a green tint would read as success on every bar.


## Hooks

### `useBand(id, override?)` — `src/hooks/use-band.ts`

The resolved palette for the current scheme, following the same order as
`useTheme` (presentation `ThemeScope`, then the saved preference, then the OS).
Referentially stable. `useBandScheme()` returns `'light' | 'dark'`.

```tsx
const band = useBand('bills');
<View style={{ backgroundColor: band.band }} />
```

### `usePatternTiles()` — `src/hooks/use-pattern.ts`

The signed-in person's pattern tiles. Subscribes only to the pattern's derived
input (name, goals, watched categories, reminder presence), never to money.

## `BandScaffold` — `src/components/ui/band-scaffold.tsx`

ScreenScaffold's responsibilities (scroll, safe areas, pull to refresh, tab-bar
clearance, keyboard, large text, testIDs) for a band screen. The band scrolls
away with the content; the status-bar strip stays the band colour; the top
overscroll shows the band and the bottom overscroll the sheet; the status bar
style follows the focused band. The sheet rises 40pt on first mount where
motion is allowed. `ScreenScaffold` is unchanged.

Screens using it hide the native stack header (`headerShown: false`); the nav
row replaces it. It must render inside a navigator (it reads `useIsFocused` to
decide which screen owns the status bar); onboarding steps outside the router
should compose `BandHeader` directly. `keyboardAware` avoids the keyboard with
one mechanism per platform (KeyboardAvoidingView on iOS, measured padding on
Android).

```tsx
<BandScaffold
  band="bills"
  testID="bills-screen"                  // also bills-screen-band / bills-screen-sheet
  tabbed                                  // clears the tab bar
  refreshControl={<CaptureRefreshControl />}   // tinted onBand automatically
  nav={{ title: t('tabBills'), actions: [{ icon: 'plus', label: words.addBill, onPress: addBill }] }}
  bandContent={<BandFigure label={words.due30} fils={total} palette={band} qualifier={words.count(n)} />}>
  {/* sheet content */}
</BandScaffold>
```

Props: `band`, `nav` (`back` true | fn, `close`, `title`, `actions`,
`leading`, `trailing`), `bandContent`, `children` (sheet), `footer` (pinned on
the sheet colour), `scroll` (false for a sheet that owns a virtualized list),
`scrollRef`, `scrollProps`, `refreshControl`, `keyboardAware`,
`keyboardVerticalOffset`, `tabbed`, `floatingClearance`, `contentStyle`,
`bandStyle`, `testID`, `bandTestID`, `sheetTestID`, `scheme`.

Also exported: `BandHeader` and `BandSheetCap` (compose a band as a
`FlatList` header and keep it scrolling away), `BandNavRow`, `BandIconButton`,
`useBandBottomInset({ tabbed, floatingClearance })` (bottom padding for a
`scroll={false}` sheet's own list), `BAND_GUTTER` (20).

## Band-level components — `src/components/ui/band/`

### `BandFigure` — `band-figure.tsx`

Label, the amount in Geist SemiBold tabular digits with a demoted currency, an
optional qualifier. Formats exactly like `Money` (minor units, device
conventions, currency placement); speaks "label, CUR amount. qualifier";
fits the amount at normal and large text sizes, putting currency on its own line when the pair cannot fit.
`rolling` rolls only changed digits (Home's Today), static under Reduce Motion
and on Android. Sizes: `hero` 56, `large` 32, `medium` 22.

```tsx
<BandFigure label={words.spentThisMonth} fils={totalMinor} moneySpec={moneySpec}
  qualifier={words.dayOf(25, 30)} palette={band} />
```

### `BandSegmented` — `band-segmented.tsx`

Tablist on a band: track in the band tone, selected pill = sheet surface with
band-colour text (ink pill + cream text on ochre and sand). Stacks at large text.

```tsx
<BandSegmented palette={band} label={words.view} value={view} onChange={setView}
  segments={[{ value: 'categories', label: words.categories }, { value: 'compare', label: words.compare }]} />
```

### `BandChip` — `band-chip.tsx`

38pt pill; a button reporting `selected` when it has `onPress`, plain text otherwise.

```tsx
<BandChip palette={band} label={words.all} selected={filter === 'all'} onPress={() => setFilter('all')} />
```

### `StatTile` — `stat-tile.tsx`

A figure tile in the band's own tone (`tone="band"`) or the logo green (`tone="accent"`).
`statTileColors(palette, tone)` gives the colours for a figure inside it.

```tsx
<StatTile palette={band} tone="accent" label={w.leftToSpend} meta={meta} accessibilityLabel={spoken}>
  <BandFigure fils={left} palette={band} size="large" color={statTileColors(band, 'accent').fg} />
</StatTile>
```

### `ShareBar` — `share-bar.tsx`

A row of coins lying on their side (face toward the start edge, so it mirrors
under RTL), one per segment, sized by its exact value. Each coin is its
segment's own `color` (Spending passes `categoryColor`); a segment without one
falls back to the band's text colour at a rank-based strength (`coinFill`).
There are at most seven coins: past that, the smallest categories share one
"N other categories" coin in `restColor`, so the row never runs off a narrow
band. Under the coins, a two-column legend (one column at large text sizes)
names every coin with its amount (`formatValue`) and share. The bar is spoken
as one image: the top three named with their shares, the rest as one figure.

Motion: coins rise into place 45ms apart with one small bounce, each with a
faint glow of its colour on the band. Tapping a coin or its legend line pops
the coin up 10pt over a brighter glow and dims the other coins and lines; tap
it again to clear. The selection is visual only, and the spoken summary does
not change. All of this is paint only (opacity and transform on the native
driver), so Android animates it too. Reduce Motion, or a running screen
reader, shows everything in place.

```tsx
<ShareBar palette={band} label={words.spending} formatValue={moneyLabel} restColor={CATEGORY_REST}
  segments={rows.map((row) => ({ key: row.category, label: categoryLabel(row.category), value: row.spentFils,
    color: categoryColor(row.category) }))} />
```

Onboarding also moves without coins, all from the person's own answers: the
Name tile flips over (rotateY) to their initial; Goals shows a mini Home whose
first sections reorder as goals are picked (`homeOrderForGoals`, the same order
Continue saves); Reminders drops bell pins onto the days Wafra really sends
reminders (`reminderPins`, from `src/lib/reminders.ts`); the pattern reveal
assembles group by group and tilts up from flat. Step changes keep the step
just left on screen until the next one has arrived (`StepLayer`), so no frame
is an empty band. Every step's primary action is pinned above the bottom safe
area while its content scrolls (`EStepFrame`), except the paywall, whose
purchase action stays with its price, terms and legal links (`pinActions={false}`).

Rules: `filledCoins(share, total)` (`src/lib/coin-math.ts`) never draws a
little money as none or nearly all as all. Empty places use
`ghostTone(surface, ink)` so stacked empty coins stay separate coins. Stacks
cast a soft contact shadow (`CoinColors.shadow`) and land with one small
squash. Coins are hidden from assistive tech; spoken labels never change.
Motion is core `Animated` on the native driver (paint only); Reduce Motion or
a running screen reader shows the final state at once, and nothing starts
before `useMotionPreference().ready`. On the web build react-native-web
reports a screen reader as always on, so the coins rest in place there; judge
motion on a device.

Widget coins are drawn natively from the snapshot (`src/lib/widget-coins.ts`
is the reference arithmetic; `WafraCoinMath` in `targets/widget`, and
`WidgetGraphics.kt` on Android, mirror it, as SwiftUI shapes and Canvas
bitmaps). `CoinColors` is mirrored once per platform (`WafraCoinPalette`,
`WidgetGraphics.COIN_*`). Category colours travel in the snapshot; a snapshot
without them keeps the share bar. Widgets never animate on their own: on
iOS 17 only the step between two timeline entries moves (numeric digit
rolls, a new coin's entrance, the Today flip), never while redacted, and Reduce
Motion drops the motion. Tinted or clear Home Screens keep only opacity, so
there the Today stack is left out and Spending keeps the share bar. Coins
are `accessibilityHidden` on iOS and not important for accessibility on
Android; every widget's spoken labels are unchanged.

### `WeekTiles` — `week-tiles.tsx`

Day bars from real values: today in the logo green, others in `bandMark`, exact currency

rows; zeros and fractional units stay visible. Bars grow 50ms apart.

```tsx
<WeekTiles palette={band} moneySpec={moneySpec} accessibilityLabel={weekSpoken}
  days={week.map((d) => ({ key: d.dateISO, label: w.weekday(d.weekday), spokenLabel: w.weekdayFull(d.weekday), fils: d.fils, today: d.today }))} />
```

### `PinTimeline` — `pin-timeline.tsx`

The next N days (default 30): baseline, day marks, a merchant-logo pin per
payment (up to eight drawn; all spoken in date order). `pinPosition()` is pure.

```tsx
<PinTimeline palette={band} pins={items.map((i) => ({ key: i.id, dayOffset: i.daysLeft, title: i.title,
  spokenAmount: `${currency} ${formatMinorUnits(i.amountFils, moneySpec)}` }))} />
```

### `DialLimit` — `dial-limit.tsx`

Circular limit dial, `accessibilityRole="adjustable"` with increment/decrement
actions, ± steppers, drag to set. The step is currency-scaled
(`ledgerTypicalMinor(25)`); `snapDialValue`/`dialValueAt` are pure. `on="band"`
for a dial set on a band (onboarding), default on the sheet.

```tsx

```

A category glyph on the sheet's one glyph ground in the text colour, one tone


Share of a limit used, coloured by status only; `limitStatus` /

### `EButton` — `band/e-button.tsx`

56pt, 16pt radius, centred label. `primary` (band `fill`, or `color={{ fill, text }}`),

```tsx
  accessibilityLabel={t('editEntry')} onPress={edit} />
```

### Sheet density

A sheet is read at a glance. On a 390 × 844 phone at the default text size its
key content and primary action are visible without scrolling; at 1.3× text it
takes at most one short scroll.

- `BottomSheet` keeps one rhythm: 12pt between blocks, a compact header (8pt
  under the title row), 12pt above a pinned footer and only 16pt plus the
  safe-area inset under it. Sheets may rise to 92% of the screen.
- A sheet's header is a 40–48pt logo or glyph tile beside its name, not a
  large tile above it. Its amount is `BandFigure size="large"`; `hero` belongs
  to a band.
- Footers are one row: the way out (quiet, at the start) and the primary (at
  the end). A row of three uses `size="compact"`. Two actions of uneven width
  use `flexDirection: 'row-reverse'` with `flexWrap: 'wrap'`, the primary first
  in the source: when they cannot share a line the primary's line comes first,
  and no label ever wraps inside its button. At the accessibility sizes the
  actions stack and scroll with the content.
- Choice and answer rows are 56–64pt with 10pt vertical padding; read-only
  fact rows (`KeyValueRows`) are 44pt.

## The personal pattern

### `buildPattern(input)` — `src/lib/pattern.ts` (pure)

A deterministic tile list on a 6 × 2 grid from `PatternInput`
`{ name, goals: GoalId[], watched: CategoryId[], reminders: { bills, cards, dailySummary } }`
— no money field exists. Composition is the boards' `M_NAME` / `M_GOALS` /
`M_WATCH` / `M_REMIND` (no bank tiles): the name's initial on clay at (0,0)
(a plain clay square when there is no name); each goal owns a fixed cell and
shape (bills quarter, salary circle, subscriptions ring, cash-cards half,
spend-less clay quarter); up to two watched categories as glyph tiles on
sand; reminder dots, ring and bars when bills, card statements or the daily
summary have something to remind about. Same answers, same pattern, in any
order; a missing answer leaves its cell empty. Tiles carry palette tokens only.
`patternInputFromState(state)` maps a ledger (placeholder name → none;
watched = categories with a monthly limit in a fixed order, so saving a limit
never redraws the pattern; never their amounts).

Home uses `compactPattern` to close gaps between existing tiles for incomplete
profiles: up to six tiles form one row, larger patterns use two balanced rows.
It preserves all recorded choices and the complete 12-cell composition. A
nameless tile uses the original Wafra mark in Home's compact presentation.
Onboarding's reveal, the paywall backdrop and the hand-off into Home use the
same compact packing, so the person lands on the picture they were shown; the
lock screen, recap cover and Welcome's example keep the fixed grid.

### `PatternMosaic` — `src/components/ui/pattern-mosaic.tsx`

Draws tiles (Views + the category `Icon`), scalable with `tile`/`gap`, one
image called "Your pattern" (`bandCopy`), static by default; `animate` pops
tiles in 70ms apart, still under Reduce Motion, screen readers and Android.
The opt-in `wrapTile(tile, drawn)` lets onboarding's reveal (`e-pattern.tsx`)

`YourPattern` (`src/components/ui/your-pattern.tsx`) is the ledger-subscribed

```tsx

```

### Goals state

`GoalId = 'salary' | 'bills' | 'subscriptions' | 'spend-less' | 'cash-cards'`
(`src/lib/types.ts`, with `GOAL_IDS` and `sanitizeGoalIds`). Stored as
**`AppState.wafraGoals?: GoalId[]`** — not `goals`, which already holds savings
goals with money in them. Set with the store action `setGoals(goals)`.
Optional and migration-safe (absent on older ledgers), sanitized on hydrate and
restore (unknown ids dropped, each id once, canonical order), validated in
backups as short code ids only (so a newer build's backup still restores here),
and kept from the phone when a restored backup predates it.

## Tab bar

- Android/web (`src/components/tab-bar.tsx`): floating ink pill (64pt, 32pt

  icon-only with `accessibilityLabel`, and holding one shows its name. At the

  constraint). The bar measures itself for `useTabBarClearance`.

  Liquid Glass.

Owner decision: "I want to see spend and a small trend if up or down, in and

Settings), the greeting, the **Spent hero** and the **transactions**. Every
other section (Today and Left in budgets, the money overview, the last seven

- **Layout reset.** `home-widget-preferences.ts` stores a layout version
  (`HOME_WIDGETS_LAYOUT_VERSION = 3`). A layout saved before v3 reads as the
  new default once, for new and existing people alike; nothing is deleted and
  every earlier section is one switch away in Customize Home. A section missing
  from a saved layout returns switched off when the default has it off. Goals
  chosen in onboarding still order the sheet and now also switch on the
  sections they promote (`homeOrderForGoals`): picking Bills is asking for Due
  soon and Coming up. The goal-order repair retires itself for pre-v3 layouts.
- **Spent hero** (`src/components/home/home-hero.tsx`). "Spent in October" as
  the band's hero figure (`BandFigure`), the month from the app period (the
  running month unless another month is chosen). The trend chip compares the
  month so far with the same days of last month (`projectHeroMonth`,
  `comparablePreviousPeriod`: salary-day months, never past the earlier
  month's end); no spending recorded in that window means no chip, never
  "+100%". Down is the accent green; over last month's pace the chip wears the
  warning colours (`statusNear` on `statusNearSoft`) and crossfades into them
  when the crossing happens while Home is open. Home gives one short haptic
  (`captured()`) when it sees the crossing, at most once a day
  (`nextPaceMemory`, stored in `wafra/ui/home-pace-nudge/v1`). In · Net sit under
  the line (Net = In − Spent, signed); larger text stacks them. Spent, In, Net
  and the categories are `summarizeMonth`, the same projection the dashboard
  and Spending use. The card speaks one label: "Spent in October, AED …, up 12%
  versus the same days of September. In AED …. Net −AED …".
- **Last month, locally.** Swipe the card sideways (`PanResponder`; mirrored
  right to left), press the chevron, or use increment/decrement: the card
  turns to last month (its own figures and its trend against the month before)
  and back. Home's period does not change. Last month is computed only when
  asked for.
- **Tilt.** The card leans at most 4° with the phone (`home-tilt.ts`: movement
  from a slowly settling resting hold, smoothed, ~30 updates a second, mirrored
  right to left), set on Animated values the card reads on the native driver.
  It needs expo-sensors' DeviceMotion native module. This build does **not**
  add `expo-sensors`: the package brings Android's `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`
  permission and an iOS `NSMotionUsageDescription` with it, both store-review
  surfaces for a decorative effect. `use-device-tilt.ts` looks the module up
  with `requireOptionalNativeModule('ExponentDeviceMotion')`, so the card stays
  flat until a build includes it; no JS change is needed then. Never on the
  web, under Reduce Motion or a screen reader, while the app is in the
  background or Home is not focused.
- **Transactions** (`home-recent.tsx`): the period's latest 12 rows grouped by
  day under a quiet label (no day totals), "See all" to Transactions. On Home
  each row is one target that opens the detail sheet (`TransactionRow
  merchantLinks={false}`); the merchant page is reached from the sheet. An
  arriving capture keeps its coin drop (`ArrivingRow`).
- **Needs you** (`needs-you-pill.tsx`, `home-needs-you.ts`): one floating pill
  above the tab bar, only when something truly needs the person, highest
  priority first: tracking paused or stopped (trial ended, permission lost,
  iPhone capture not set up, alerts stopped arriving; a deliberate opt-out is
  never nagged) > an overdue card payment > transfers to answer (the Transfers
  question queue) > a monthly limit over. A tap goes where it is answered; ×
  hides that need for the session. The sheet pads its end so the pill never
  covers the last row; on Android it stops short of the Add button.
- **Motion** follows the coin rules: core Animated on the native driver, one
  clock per group (`useCoinClock`), no JS timers for staggers, held until Home
  can be seen (`useHomeRevealReady`), final state under Reduce Motion or a
  screen reader, nothing while the app is inactive. Decorative pieces (line,
  chip, coins) are hidden from assistive tech.
