# Design language E — the foundation

Owner-approved on 2026-09-25. This file is the contract every screen workstream
builds on: the rules, the band each screen wears, the dark-mode mapping, and
every foundation API with a short example. Screens **use** these pieces; if one
is missing, add it in your own files and say so — do not fork the foundation.

Reference boards (design data, not code): `App-*.dc.html` and `App-*-Dark.dc.html`,
generated by `boards_app0.py`, `boards_app1.py`, `boards_app2.py`, `boards_e.py`
and `dark.py`. Home (`src/screens/journal-home-screen.tsx`) is the worked
example of everything below.

## The rules

- **Every tab owns a colour.** Home ink, Spending clay, Bills ochre, Accounts
  slate. Flows (capture, review, add) are green; settings and data are sand.
  Detail screens take their parent's colour.
- **A screen is a band and a sheet.** The band holds the one figure that
  matters; the cream sheet (28pt top radius, overlapping the band by 28pt)
  holds the detail. Sheets and dialogs are the same surface, lifted.
- **Geist only.** No serif, no italics. Band figures are Geist SemiBold with
  tabular digits (`BandFigure`) — never Geist Mono, whose comma spaces out
  "5 , 480". Row amounts keep Geist Mono (`Money`, `TransactionRow`).
  Headlines say what the screen is or shows; no slogans.
- **One accent per screen** (its tab colour) plus mint for the one figure that
  is good news. Stat tiles use the band's own tone.
- **Colour in lists means status only**: green within a limit, amber from 85%,
  red over it (`StatusBar`, `limitStatus`). Never a category's identity —
  category glyph tiles are one tone (`GlyphTile`).
- **Everything drawn is a tile from real figures**: merchant logo tiles (kept
  on every merchant row; the category glyph is only the fallback), glyph
  tiles, week bars, calendar cells, the share bar, timeline pins. No
  decorative shapes, no invented data.
- **The personal pattern** (`PatternMosaic`) appears only on onboarding,
  Home's header, the lock screen and the recap cover. It never carries money.
- **Tab bar.** Android and web: a floating ink pill; the selected tab shows its
  colour and name, the others are spoken icons. iOS: the system tab bar
  (NativeTabs), each item tinted with its tab colour.
- **Buttons** are plain: 56pt, 16pt radius, centred label, no arrow in a
  circle (`EButton`). Chips are 38pt pills (`BandChip`).
- **Motion.** Sheets rise 40pt on the 260/24 spring; bands never move; bars
  and pins grow from the baseline 50ms apart; money never spins. Reduce
  Motion (or a running screen reader) shows everything in place. Android keeps
  its measured motion bypasses.
- **Large text.** Every band and sheet layout stacks or wraps under
  `useLargeTextLayout()` (see `2026-09-25-large-text-audit.md`).
- **RTL.** Bands and sheets mirror (logical `start`/`end` everywhere; the back
  chevron flips in `Icon`). Figures stay LTR-isolated as today.
- **Honesty.** BRIEF.md's rules still apply in full: never invent data, never
  merge incomplete balances, never claim coverage the app does not have. No
  English sentence in `src/app` or `src/components`: copy lives in `src/lib/*-copy.ts`.

## Band for each screen

| Band id | Colour (light / dark) | Screens |
|---|---|---|
| `home` | ink `#16130F` / `#0B0A08` | Home, Transactions, TxDetail, Filter, Ask, ApplePay, CustomizeHome, Lock |
| `spending` | clay `#A4432F` / `#6E2B1E` | Spending (the `flow` tab), Merchant, Merchants, Currencies, Limit |
| `bills` | ochre `#E2B45A` / `#5E4719` | Bills, BillDetail |
| `accounts` | slate `#2F6577` / `#1B3F4A` | Accounts (the `wallet` tab), AccountDetail, Card, CardPayment, Transfers, Trusted |
| `flow` | green `#1F6B52` / `#14473A` | Add, Review, CaptureStatus, Goal, Feedback, AndroidSms |
| `settings` | sand `#EDE3CF` / `#26221C` | Settings, DataHelp, ImproveCategories, Statements, NotFound |

Note the naming trap: the **`flow` tab route** is Spending and wears the
`spending` band; the **`flow` band id** is the green capture/review colour.
`TAB_BANDS` in `src/components/tab-bar.tsx` maps tab routes to band ids.

## Dark mode mapping (dark.py)

- Bands deepen so they never glare: ink `#0B0A08`, clay `#6E2B1E`, ochre
  `#5E4719` (light text on it), slate `#1B3F4A`, green `#14473A`, sand `#26221C`.
  Text on every dark band is `#F2EFE8`.
- Sheets `#1C1A16`, cards `#24211C`, rules `#3B362E`, text `#F2EFE8`,
  secondary `#A9A29A`, glyph ground `#2E2A23`.
- Glyphs, links and status move to lighter tints on dark surfaces: clay
  `#E08A70`, slate `#7FB4C4`, green `#57B894`, amber `#D9AE62` (`tint`,
  `statusOk/Near/Over`).
- Controls keep their accent fill; text set on an accent keeps its colour.
- The pattern keeps its fills on the ink band; sand grounds become `#2E2A23`
  and glyph strokes take the lighter tints (`PatternPalette.dark`).
- The segmented control's selected pill becomes the dark sheet with the band
  tint as its text.

## Tokens — `src/constants/theme.ts`

Additive: `Colors`, `Fonts`, `Radius`, `Motion` and the Ledger & Light baseline
are unchanged (`node scripts/check-design-baseline.mjs`).

- `BandId`, `BAND_IDS`
- `BandPalette` — per band and scheme: `band`, `onBand`, `onBandSecondary`,
  `tile` (the band's own tone: stat tiles, segmented track, chips, round
  actions), `bandRule`, `bandMark` (non-today bars), `sheet`, `card`, `rule`,
  `glyphGround`, `text`, `textSecondary`, `tint` (the band colour as it reads
  on the sheet), `fill`/`onFill` (primary button), `selected`/`onSelected`
  (segmented pill), `accent`/`onAccent`/`onAccentSecondary` (mint),
  `statusOk`/`statusNear`/`statusOver` and their `…Soft` tints, `statusBar`
  (`'light' | 'dark'`), `tabTint` (iOS selected tab).
  Every text token is solid and tested at 4.5:1 where it is drawn
  (`scripts/test/repair/band-palette.test.cjs`).
- `BandPalettes.light|dark[id]`, `bandPalette(id, scheme)` — pure access.
- `BandLayout` — `sheetRadius` 28, `sheetOverlap` 28, `sheetRise` 40,
  `buttonHeight` 56, `buttonRadius` 16, `chipHeight` 38.
- `TabPill.light|dark` — the floating tab bar's colours.
- `PatternColor`, `PatternPalette.light|dark.{fill,mark}`.

```ts
import { bandPalette } from '@/constants/theme';
const clay = bandPalette('spending', 'light'); // clay.band === '#A4432F'
```

## Hooks

### `useBand(id, override?)` — `src/hooks/use-band.ts`

The resolved palette for the current scheme, following the same order as
`useTheme` (presentation `ThemeScope`, then the saved preference, then the OS).
Referentially stable. `useBandScheme()` returns `'light' | 'dark'`.

```tsx
const band = useBand('bills');
<View style={{ backgroundColor: band.band }} />
```

### `usePatternTiles()` — `src/hooks/use-pattern.ts`

The signed-in person's pattern tiles. Subscribes only to the pattern's derived
input (name, goals, watched categories, reminder presence), never to money.

## `BandScaffold` — `src/components/ui/band-scaffold.tsx`

ScreenScaffold's responsibilities (scroll, safe areas, pull to refresh, tab-bar
clearance, keyboard, large text, testIDs) for a band screen. The band scrolls
away with the content; the status-bar strip stays the band colour; the top
overscroll shows the band and the bottom overscroll the sheet; the status bar
style follows the focused band. The sheet rises 40pt on first mount where
motion is allowed. `ScreenScaffold` is unchanged.

Screens using it hide the native stack header (`headerShown: false`); the nav
row replaces it.

```tsx
<BandScaffold
  band="bills"
  testID="bills-screen"                  // also bills-screen-band / bills-screen-sheet
  tabbed                                  // clears the tab bar
  refreshControl={<CaptureRefreshControl />}   // tinted onBand automatically
  nav={{ title: t('tabBills'), actions: [{ icon: 'plus', label: words.addBill, onPress: addBill }] }}
  bandContent={<BandFigure label={words.due30} fils={total} palette={band} qualifier={words.count(n)} />}>
  {/* sheet content */}
</BandScaffold>
```

Props: `band`, `nav` (`back` true | fn, `close`, `title`, `actions`,
`leading`, `trailing`), `bandContent`, `children` (sheet), `footer` (pinned on
the sheet colour), `scroll` (false for a sheet that owns a virtualized list),
`scrollRef`, `scrollProps`, `refreshControl`, `keyboardAware`,
`keyboardVerticalOffset`, `tabbed`, `floatingClearance`, `contentStyle`,
`bandStyle`, `testID`, `bandTestID`, `sheetTestID`, `scheme`.

Also exported: `BandHeader` and `BandSheetCap` (compose a band as a
`FlatList` header and keep it scrolling away), `BandNavRow`, `BandIconButton`,
`useBandBottomInset({ tabbed, floatingClearance })` (bottom padding for a
`scroll={false}` sheet's own list), `BAND_GUTTER` (20).

## Band-level components — `src/components/ui/band/`

### `BandFigure` — `band-figure.tsx`

Label, the amount in Geist SemiBold tabular digits with a demoted currency, an
optional qualifier. Formats exactly like `Money` (minor units, device
conventions, currency placement); speaks "label, CUR amount. qualifier";
shrinks toward its width at large text and puts the currency on its own line.
`rolling` rolls only changed digits (Home's Today), static under Reduce Motion
and on Android. Sizes: `hero` 56, `large` 32, `medium` 22.

```tsx
<BandFigure label={words.spentThisMonth} fils={totalMinor} moneySpec={moneySpec}
  qualifier={words.dayOf(25, 30)} palette={band} />
```

### `BandSegmented` — `band-segmented.tsx`

Tablist on a band: track in the band tone, selected pill = sheet surface with
band-colour text (ink pill + cream text on ochre and sand). Stacks at large text.

```tsx
<BandSegmented palette={band} label={words.view} value={view} onChange={setView}
  segments={[{ value: 'categories', label: words.categories }, { value: 'compare', label: words.compare }]} />
```

### `BandChip` — `band-chip.tsx`

38pt pill; a button reporting `selected` when it has `onPress`, plain text otherwise.

```tsx
<BandChip palette={band} label={words.all} selected={filter === 'all'} onPress={() => setFilter('all')} />
```

### `StatTile` — `stat-tile.tsx`

A figure tile in the band's own tone (`tone="band"`) or mint (`tone="accent"`).
`statTileColors(palette, tone)` gives the colours for a figure inside it.

```tsx
<StatTile palette={band} tone="accent" label={w.leftToSpend} meta={meta} accessibilityLabel={spoken}>
  <BandFigure fils={left} palette={band} size="large" color={statTileColors(band, 'accent').fg} />
</StatTile>
```

### `ShareBar` — `share-bar.tsx`

One tone split by share (rank-based strength of `onBand`), the top three named
with whole-number shares that add to 100 (`sharePercents`), "+N more" for the
rest; spoken as one image. Shares only, never amounts.

```tsx
<ShareBar palette={band} label={words.spending}
  segments={rows.map((row) => ({ key: row.category, label: categoryLabel(row.category), value: row.spentFils }))} />
```

### `WeekTiles` — `week-tiles.tsx`

Day bars from real values: today in mint, others in `bandMark`, whole-unit
values above each bar (they step aside at large text), bars grow 50ms apart.

```tsx
<WeekTiles palette={band} moneySpec={moneySpec} accessibilityLabel={weekSpoken}
  days={week.map((d) => ({ key: d.dateISO, label: w.weekday(d.weekday), spokenLabel: w.weekdayFull(d.weekday), fils: d.fils, today: d.today }))} />
```

### `PinTimeline` — `pin-timeline.tsx`

The next N days (default 30): baseline, day marks, a merchant-logo pin per
payment (up to eight drawn; all spoken in date order). `pinPosition()` is pure.

```tsx
<PinTimeline palette={band} pins={items.map((i) => ({ key: i.id, dayOffset: i.daysLeft, title: i.title,
  spokenAmount: `${currency} ${formatMinorUnits(i.amountFils, moneySpec)}` }))} />
```

### `DialLimit` — `dial-limit.tsx`

Circular limit dial, `accessibilityRole="adjustable"` with increment/decrement
actions, ± steppers, drag to set. The step is currency-scaled
(`ledgerTypicalMinor(25)`); `snapDialValue`/`dialValueAt` are pure. `on="band"`
for a dial set on a band (onboarding), default on the sheet.

```tsx
<DialLimit label={categoryLabel('dining')} valueMinor={limit} onChange={setLimit} palette={band} moneySpec={moneySpec} />
```

## Sheet-level components

### `GlyphTile` — `band/glyph-tile.tsx`

A category glyph on the sheet's one glyph ground in the text colour — one tone
for every category.

```tsx
<GlyphTile category="groceries" palette={band} size={40} />
```

### `StatusBar` (limit bar) — `band/status-bar.tsx`

Share of a limit used, coloured by status only; `limitStatus` /
`limitFillPercent` live in `src/lib/limit-status.ts` (85% near, over past
100%). Also exported as `LimitStatusBar` and `limitStatusColor(palette, status)`.
Not the system status bar.

```tsx
<StatusBar spentMinor={row.spentFils} limitMinor={row.limitFils} palette={band} />
```

### `EButton` — `band/e-button.tsx`

56pt, 16pt radius, centred label. `primary` (band `fill`, or `color={{ fill, text }}`),
`secondary` (card with a rule), `quiet` (tint text). `busy` shows a spinner.

```tsx
<EButton palette={band} label={words.recordPayment} onPress={record} />
<EButton palette={band} variant="quiet" label={words.details} onPress={open} />
```

## The personal pattern

### `buildPattern(input)` — `src/lib/pattern.ts` (pure)

A deterministic tile list on a 6 × 2 grid from `PatternInput`
`{ name, goals: GoalId[], watched: CategoryId[], reminders: { bills, cards, dailySummary } }`
— no money field exists. Composition is the boards' `M_NAME` / `M_GOALS` /
`M_WATCH` / `M_REMIND` (no bank tiles): the name's initial on clay at (0,0)
(a plain clay square when there is no name); each goal owns a fixed cell and
shape (bills quarter, salary circle, subscriptions ring, cash-cards half,
spend-less clay quarter); up to two watched categories as glyph tiles on
sand; reminder dots, ring and bars when bills, card statements or the daily
summary have something to remind about. Same answers, same pattern, in any
order; a missing answer leaves its cell empty. Tiles carry palette tokens only.
`patternInputFromState(state)` maps a ledger (placeholder name → none;
watched = categories with a monthly limit, their order not their amounts).

### `PatternMosaic` — `src/components/ui/pattern-mosaic.tsx`

Draws tiles (Views + the category `Icon`), scalable with `tile`/`gap`, one
image called "Your pattern" (`bandCopy`), static by default; `animate` pops
tiles in 70ms apart, still under Reduce Motion, screen readers and Android.
`YourPattern` (`src/components/ui/your-pattern.tsx`) is the ledger-subscribed
version for Home, the lock screen and the recap cover.

```tsx
<YourPattern tile={24} gap={3} />                       // Home's header
<PatternMosaic tiles={buildPattern(answersSoFar)} animate />   // onboarding
```

### Goals state

`GoalId = 'salary' | 'bills' | 'subscriptions' | 'spend-less' | 'cash-cards'`
(`src/lib/types.ts`, with `GOAL_IDS` and `sanitizeGoalIds`). Stored as
**`AppState.wafraGoals?: GoalId[]`** — not `goals`, which already holds savings
goals with money in them. Set with the store action `setGoals(goals)`.
Optional and migration-safe (absent on older ledgers), sanitized on hydrate and
restore, validated in backups (`wafraGoals`, known ids once each), and kept
from the phone when a restored backup predates it.

## Tab bar

- Android/web (`src/components/tab-bar.tsx`): floating ink pill (64pt, 32pt
  radius, 16pt from the edges, above the safe area). The selected tab is a pill
  in its tab colour with its name (`TAB_BANDS`, `TabPill`); the others are
  icon-only with `accessibilityLabel`, and holding one shows its name. At the
  accessibility sizes every tab is icon-only. Drawn, never animated (perf
  constraint). The bar measures itself for `useTabBarClearance`.
- iOS (`src/components/app-tabs-layout.ios.tsx`): NativeTabs kept; each
  trigger sets `Icon selectedColor` and `Label selectedStyle.color` to its
  band's `tabTint` (expo-router 55 applies these per item). The bar-wide
  `tintColor` is Home's tint as a fallback. Needs a device check on iOS 26
  Liquid Glass.

## Copy

`src/lib/band-copy.ts` (`bandCopy(language)`): the labels the shared pieces
speak on their own (Back, Close, Your pattern, shares, day counts, stepper
labels). English and Arabic parity is tested (`band-copy.test.cjs`).
