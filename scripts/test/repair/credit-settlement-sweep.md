No customer messages are included.

`credit-settlement-closure.test.cjs` exercises the fixtures and focused controls through parseSms, createLaunchAlertSession and the committed native JS bundle:763 tests. Every uncertain-credit row in the original sweep must reach informational app Review and native Review with a transaction money role. Informational events cannot be confirmed into the ledger. The additional capture/history suite tests the real source-free and historical import paths, not only parser facts.

Expected directions and minor units are explicit. A request process's success cannot establish completed money. A request beside an independent debit cannot turn that debit into income. An unposted genuine-but-uncertain credit is accepted only when retained as informational Review on app/native. Native's existing ignored-debit policy and six AlRajhi AE/AED country-mismatch controls remain explicit. The existing unsafe-link and two-movement exceptions (DB14, CR12/#238) are documented in their rows.

The ordinary repair CI discovers both test files; native assertions are unconditional. Existing financial-refusal tests still forbid automatic posting. Where financial evidence is retained instead of ignored, those tests now require informational status and refusal by the actual confirmed-import planner; authentication, link and other unsafe-message expectations remain strict.

Review input is retained in Shared/wafra-cross-family-sweep-20261114, Shared/wafra-closure-review-5dac08dc, Shared/wafra-reviewer-closure-5dac08dc and Shared/wafra-closure-review-5dac08dc-change-reviewer.
